Fitness and Nutrition Courses - Group 23

Course Introduction

Group 23 offers specialized courses in fitness and nutrition to help you improve your health and build a balanced lifestyle.

Fitness Course

This course is designed to improve physical fitness, enhance strength, and boost endurance. It includes:

  • Strength and resistance training
  • Aerobic and cardio workouts
  • Customized training plans
  • Injury prevention techniques

Healthy Nutrition Course

This course provides essential knowledge about healthy nutrition, including:

  • Basics of nutrition and essential nutrients
  • Healthy meal planning
  • Weight management and achieving health goals
  • Relationship between food and energy

Course Duration

The courses last between 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the level of specialization required.

Certification and Accreditation

Upon successful completion of the course, trainees receive a certified certificate from Group 23.
