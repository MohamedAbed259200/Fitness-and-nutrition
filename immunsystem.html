Immune System

What is the Immune System?

The immune system is a complex network of cells, tissues, and organs that work together to protect the body from microbes, viruses, and parasites.

Components of the Immune System

  • White blood cells: The first line of defense against infections.
  • Spleen: Filters the blood and removes germs and damaged cells.
  • Lymph nodes: Help produce immune cells.
  • Bone marrow: Produces white blood cells necessary to fight infections.

How Does the Immune System Work?

When a foreign body enters, the immune system responds by producing antibodies that attack and eliminate the intruder.

How to Boost the Immune System

  • Follow a healthy diet rich in vitamins and minerals.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Get enough sleep.
  • Reduce stress and mental strain.
